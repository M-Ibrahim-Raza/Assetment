# Assetment

A personal finance web app for people in Pakistan. It covers two areas, both Must for v1: **expense tracking** and **asset tracking**. Totals are in PKR. Anyone can sign up, and each account is private. Web only, responsive on phone and desktop.

## Problem

People who track their money in a spreadsheet run into these problems. The last time they wanted to know their total assets or where their money went, they updated the spreadsheet by hand.

- **Logging is a chore.** Adding each expense by hand is slow, so the sheet falls behind.
- **No single net worth view.** Money sits in banks, wallets, cash, gold, stocks and property, and debts sit elsewhere. Nothing shows the full picture.
- **Net worth is never current.** Gold, stock and currency prices change, and updating them by hand is a pain.
- **Can't see where money goes.** Spending by category, or this month vs last, is hard to get.
- **No view of investment gains.** Profit or loss on stocks and funds needs manual sums.

## Persona

**Salaried investor (Pakistan, 25–40)**

- **Who:** a salaried professional. Salary lands in a bank account. Spends by card, wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa) and cash.
- **Owns and owes:** bank and wallet balances, some gold, PSX stocks or mutual funds, maybe a plot. Debts such as a loan, a card balance or a committee.
- **Trying to:** know their net worth and where their money goes.
- **Today:** a spreadsheet kept by hand.
- **Frustration:** falls behind on logging, and can't see net worth without updating many cells.

## Goal and success metric

- **Goal:** one place that keeps net worth and spending current with little effort.
- **Success metric:** today's net worth is visible within 5 seconds of opening the app.

## Appetite

6 weeks for v1. Scope is cut to fit.

## Core journeys

Steps for each are in [journeys.md](journeys.md).

- J1 – Set up and see net worth
- J2 – Log money in or out
- J3 – See where money goes
- J4 – Track investments
- J5 – Watch net worth over time

## Non-goals

v1 will not have:

- Budgets, spending limits or alerts
- Tax or FBR wealth statement reports
- Bank sync, or statement and CSV import
- Shared or household accounts
- Live prices (prices refresh daily)
- Other markets, or a base currency other than PKR
- Native mobile apps
