# J2 – Log money in or out

**Persona:** salaried investor ([product.md](../product.md))

**Goal:** record money spent, received or moved in seconds, with balances and net worth kept up to date.

| # | Step | Screen | Features |
|---|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Tap Add from any screen | Quick add | F14 |
| 2 | Pick spent, received or moved | Quick add | F14 |
| 3 | Enter the amount, or tap a usual or scan a receipt. Category and account come filled in | Quick add | F14, F15, F16, F17 |
| 4 | Check the details read from the receipt and fix any errors | Receipt review | F17 |
| 5 | Save. You're back where you were, and the account balance and net worth update | Dashboard | F01, F02, F05, F14 |
