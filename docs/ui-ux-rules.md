# UI/UX Rules

Rules for people and AI agents who design, build or review a screen.

1. **One job per screen.** One main button per screen. Rare actions go in a menu, one step away.
2. **Phone first.** Every screen works at 320 px wide and 200% zoom, with no sideways scroll.
3. **Every state.** Build loading, empty, error and success states. Errors say what to do next.
4. **Fast feedback.** Respond to every tap within 100 ms. Show progress for anything over 1 s.
5. **Less typing.** Prefill date, account and category. Use the number keypad for amounts.
6. **Forgiving forms.** Use visible labels. Show errors next to the field after the user leaves it. Keep input on failure.
7. **Undo, don't ask.** Use Undo for reversible actions. Confirm only permanent ones, with the verb on the button: "Delete account".
8. **View in the URL.** Keep filters, sort, tabs and search in the URL, so Back and reload keep the view.
9. **Clear money.** One PKR format everywhere, right-aligned digits, and + or − on gains and losses, not just color.
10. **Accessible.** Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Everything works by keyboard and touch. Nothing needs hover.
